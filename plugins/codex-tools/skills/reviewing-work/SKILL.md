# Reviewing Work

Mode: **review-only**. Load the [shared procedure](../../references/review-and-refinement.md) and [invocation guide](../../references/invocation.md).

For requested or required independent review, follow the shared opposite-family rule. If the required family or route cannot be established, stop and ask for help. Do not replace required independent review with inline inspection.

Pin the subject/revision and relevant context. Report supported findings with location, consequence, evidence, and a concrete correction or question. No findings is valid. Do not edit, delegate further when already assigned a review, or claim publication authority.
