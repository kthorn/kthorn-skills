# Refining Documents

Mode: **authorized document refinement**, not general implementation. Load the [shared procedure](../../references/review-and-refinement.md) and [invocation guide](../../references/invocation.md).

Keep requested or required independent review read-only and follow the shared opposite-family rule. If the required family or route cannot be established, stop and ask for help.

The authorized writer independently verifies findings and applies targeted document fixes. Preserve unaffected content and user decisions; consequential scope/design changes return to the operator. Stop after substantive gaps resolve, without extra ceremonial reviews or automatically marking the document refined.
